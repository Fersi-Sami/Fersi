I am currently a PhD student at the university of Paris-Saclay & IHES under the supervision of Pr. Ahmed Abbes. My PhD topic is in algebraic geometry, I work on a generalization of the Cartier Transform of Ogus and Vologodsky to log smooth schemes. My research interests include logarithmic geometry, Cartier transform, Higgs fields and integrable connections, Algebraic differential calculus...
Before switching to pure mathematics, I was an engineering student and worked on topics related to finance and economics.
